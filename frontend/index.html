QUICK GUIDE

How it works

Three simple steps to check a voice recording.

1

Choose a voice recording

Select an audio file recorded on a phone, laptop, or voice recorder (WAV, MP3, M4A, AAC, FLAC, OGG, WebM).

2

Wait while the app checks it

The app studies voice features such as pitch, pauses, energy, and rhythm.

3

Read the result and explanation

See what pattern the AI noticed and read clear, plain-language guidance on what the result means.

💡

Tips for a clear check

  • Record in a quiet room with minimal background noise.
  • Speak at a natural, comfortable speed and volume.
  • Keep the recording at least 3 seconds long so the AI has enough speech to check.

    ADReSSo 2021 Dataset Overview

    The Alzheimer's Dementia Recognition through Spontaneous Speech (ADReSSo) benchmark includes high-quality acoustic recordings of participants describing the standardized "Cookie Theft" picture.

    Total Audio Recordings 166
    Control / Typical Group 79
    Cognitive Change Group 87
    Average Recording Length 77.5 seconds

    Standardized Speech Task

    Participants were invited to freely describe everything happening in the Boston Diagnostic Aphasia Examination "Cookie Theft" image. Spontaneous speech allows the AI to evaluate natural conversational cadence, hesitations, pitch inflections, and timbre.

    ASSISTANCE

    Help & Frequently Asked Questions.

    Everything you need to know about using the voice screening tool, microphone permissions, and interpreting results.

    What does this app do?

    It analyzes the sound features of a spoken voice recording—such as pitch inflection, loudness variation, and pause lengths—to see whether they align more closely with study recordings from individuals with typical memory or individuals experiencing cognitive changes.

    Does this give a medical diagnosis?

    No. This tool is an experimental research system. It cannot confirm, diagnose, or rule out Alzheimer's disease or any medical condition. Please speak with a doctor or qualified clinician if you have health concerns.

    What kind of microphone should I use?

    Any standard built-in laptop microphone, smartphone recorder, or USB headset works well. Record in a quiet room and speak at a natural conversation volume.

    Is my recording uploaded to the internet?

    No. Audio files and screening data are processed on your computer. Your files are not shared publicly or used for advertising.

    Why does the recording need to be at least 3 seconds long?

    The AI needs enough continuous speech to measure vocal rhythm, pitch patterns, and pauses accurately.
